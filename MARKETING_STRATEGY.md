## 3. Daily Interaction & Warm-Up Schedule

To build account trust and avoid automated "bot" triggers while training the algorithm:

### Daily Warm-Up Routine (15–20 minutes/day)
1. **Morning Session (10 mins)**:
   - Open Instagram on your main mobile device (connected to home/cellular Wi-Fi without VPN).
   - Scroll through your Home Feed & Explore page.
   - Like 3–5 high-quality posts from Category A & B accounts.
   - Leave **2–3 thoughtful, multi-word comments** on recent posts (e.g., *"The auto-caption styling on this is clean, but multi-speaker reframing is where most tools fall short."*).
2. **Pre-Posting Session (5 mins before posting)**:
   - Watch 3–4 Reels in your niche.
   - React / comment on 1 post from a creator in your target audience.
3. **Post-Posting Buffer**:
   - **Do NOT close the app immediately after posting.** Stay active in the app for 5 minutes responding to any initial comments or engaging with feed posts.

### Rules to Prevent Account Shadowbans
- ❌ Do NOT follow more than 20–30 accounts per day.
- ❌ Do NOT use copy-pasted generic comments (e.g., "Nice post! 🔥", "Check out my tool").
- ❌ Do NOT switch between VPN locations or multiple devices rapidly.
- ❌ Do NOT post the exact same video 5 times in one day across different fresh accounts.

## 5. 5 Plug-and-Play Reel Content Scripts

### Script 1: Visual "Before vs After" (Problem / Solution)
- **Visual**: Split screen or quick cut showing a tedious Premiere Pro keyframe workflow vs. MakeMyClip.
- **Hook Text (0-3s)**: *"Stop keyframing 45-minute podcasts by hand."*
- **Audio / Script**:
  - *"If you edit podcasts, you know how annoying it is to manually crop horizontal videos into 9:16 vertical clips for every single speaker."*
  - *(Show video link being pasted into MakeMyClip)*
  - *"Instead, just drop your YouTube link here. The AI automatically tracks active speakers, crops the frame, and generates stylized captions line-by-line."*
  - **CTA**: *"Link in bio to try it on your next podcast episode."*

### Script 2: Technical Edge / Build-In-Public (Multi-Speaker Reframing)
- **Visual**: Show a 3-person podcast clip failing on a single-crop tool vs. MakeMyClip's layout detection.
- **Hook Text (0-3s)**: *"Why standard AI clippers fail on 3-person podcast clips..."*
- **Audio / Script**:
  - *"Most AI video cutters force a single-person center crop, which cuts off hosts when 3 or more people are talking."*

## 7. Metrics & Performance Tracking

- **Healthy Initial Reach**: 100 – 500 views on your first 3–5 Reels within 24 hours.
- **Key Signal**: Watch time / completion rate (aim for > 60% retention in first 3 seconds).
- **If views are single-digit (< 10) on 3+ posts**:
  1. Continue daily warm-up routine for 5 extra days without posting.
  2. Verify that video content has dynamic movement or hook text in the first 2 seconds.
  3. Ensure no external links exist in the post caption.
