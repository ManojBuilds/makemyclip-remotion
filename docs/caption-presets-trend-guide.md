# Caption Presets — Trending Video Type Guide

> Which caption style works for which type of content — based on current short-form trends (2024–2025).

---

## 💰 Money Mode (`hormozi`)

**Trending Video Type:** Business / Finance / Motivational talking-head

- Founder content (Alex Hormozi, Gary Vee, Codie Sanchez style)
- "How I made $X" clips, sales mindset, entrepreneurship rants
- Product pitches, offer breakdowns, cold outreach training

**Best for:**
- Face-to-camera with fast, punchy delivery
- 2 words max per cut — forces high-energy pace
- All-caps + yellow highlight on key words = instant authority

**Best Platforms:** Instagram Reels · TikTok · LinkedIn

---

## 🐉 Hype Beast (`beast`)

**Trending Video Type:** Challenge / Entertainment / Reaction

- MrBeast-style challenges, stunt videos, gaming highlights
- High-energy commentary, "I did X for 24 hours" content
- Reaction videos, prank compilations, competitive content

**Best for:**
- Action-heavy footage with lots of fast cuts
- Strong facial expressions and physical reactions
- Spring zoom animation matches high-energy pacing

**Best Platforms:** YouTube Shorts · TikTok

---

## ✨ Clean Glow (`box-highlight`)

**Trending Video Type:** Educational / Tutorial / Product Demo

- "How to" videos, app walkthroughs, clean explainers
- Professional product reviews, SaaS tool demos
- Step-by-step guides, listicles, FAQ answers

**Best for:**
- Screen recordings or clean talking-head — no busy backgrounds
- Box highlight draws eye to the active word without distracting
- Suits slower, deliberate speech pace (4 words per phrase)

**Best Platforms:** LinkedIn · Twitter/X · YouTube Shorts

---

## 🎙️ Podcast Pro (`simple`)

**Trending Video Type:** Long-form repurposed clips / Podcast / Interview

- Joe Rogan / Lex Fridman / Huberman-style podcast clips
- Expert interview snippets, debate moments, storytelling arcs
- Hot takes, controversial opinions, deep-dive discussions

**Best for:**
- 2-person setups, calm steady delivery, cerebral topics
- Lower-thirds placement feels professional and non-intrusive
- Fade-in/out suits longer phrase groups (5 words)

**Best Platforms:** LinkedIn · Twitter/X · YouTube

---

## 🚀 Viral Pop (`opus`)

**Trending Video Type:** Repurposed keynote / speech clips (Opus Clip style)

- TEDx-style talks, investor pitch moments, conference keynotes
- Surprising stat drops, bold predictions, controversial takes
- AI/tech thought leader soundbites

**Best for:**
- Single speaker with strong lighting
- Blur-in entrance animation creates instant intrigue at scroll stop
- Uppercase + cyan highlight signals tech/innovation niche

**Best Platforms:** TikTok · Instagram Reels · YouTube Shorts

---

## 🔥 Underline It (`popline`)

**Trending Video Type:** Storytelling / Narrative / Emotional moment

- Personal story videos, "here's what happened" clips
- Travel content, lifestyle vlogs, emotional hooks
- Life advice, vulnerability moments, transformation stories

**Best for:**
- B-roll with voiceover OR casual handheld talking-head
- Slide-up animation with tilt feels raw and human, not over-produced
- Orange-red highlight adds urgency without aggression

**Best Platforms:** TikTok · Instagram Reels

---

## 💜 Neon Glow (`neon-glow`)

**Trending Video Type:** Gaming / Tech / AI / Cyberpunk aesthetic

- GPU/hardware reviews, AI tool walkthroughs, coding tutorials
- Crypto/web3 content, dark-mode UI demos, game commentary
- Cyberpunk-aesthetic edits, synthwave-style content

**Best for:**
- Dark backgrounds — the glow disappears on bright footage
- Orbitron font + hot pink highlight signals sci-fi / tech niche
- Wider letter spacing suits slower, more dramatic pacing

**Best Platforms:** TikTok · YouTube Shorts · Twitch clips

## Key Trend Insights (2024–2025)

- **Sound-off optimisation** is critical — 80%+ of mobile video is watched muted. Bold captions ARE the content.
- **Word-by-word animation** outperforms static subtitles for retention across all niches.
- **2–3 words per cut** is the sweet spot for high-energy content; 4–5 words suits educational/podcast.
- **Hormozi + Beast styles** dominate raw engagement; **Simple + Clean Glow** win on professional platforms.
- **Neon Glow** is niche but extremely sticky for the gaming/tech/AI audience demographic.
